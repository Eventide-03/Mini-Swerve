Designing and Building a mini version of a Swerve drive, commonly used in FRC, FTC, and VEX Robotics competitions. Utilizing custom circuitry and software in order to replicate the FRC Environment at a smaller scale.

This swerve is 12.25x12.25 inches, and it is very tiny and cool!!!
Below are a couple screenshots of the finalized CAD, and the PCB I designed which is supposed to carry all of the electronic components.
I will be using Makerbase's ESP32 dual FOC ESC for controlling of the steering motors, and the Speedybee F405 ESC for controlling of the driving motors.
